Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Absolute Optical Sovereignty: 100% On-Device EVM Processing

BioPulse Vision operates under strict medical privacy standards. All video frames, Eulerian spatial pyramid decompositions, optical rPPG waveforms, and hemodynamic metrics are computed exclusively inside your device's local RAM and NPU/GPU hardware. At no point are video streams, camera snapshots, or biometric measurements ever recorded, cached, or transmitted to any external server or cloud service.

2. Zero Cloud Dependencies & No Internet Permission

BioPulse Vision does not declare the Android INTERNET permission in its manifest. The application has zero capability to contact remote web servers, cloud APIs, or analytics networks. It operates completely and indefinitely in Airplane Mode.

3. Camera Permission Scope

The CAMERA permission is requested exclusively to capture live optical light reflection from the user's skin for Eulerian Video Magnification and pulse extraction. Frames are processed frame-by-frame in volatile memory and immediately discarded. No video or photo files are saved to device storage.

4. Zero Telemetry, Analytics, or Advertising

The software contains zero third-party software development kits (SDKs), zero advertising trackers, and zero behavioral telemetry tools. We collect zero analytics regarding vital sign readings, session frequencies, or user physiology.

5. Policy Contact

For questions or developer inquiries regarding BioPulse Vision's optical privacy architecture, please inspect the open documentation repository or contact the development team.
